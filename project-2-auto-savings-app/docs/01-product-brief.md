# Product Brief: ForgetMeNot (Auto Round-Up Savings)

## 1. Feature Overview
ForgetMeNot is an automated micro-savings module integrated into the mobile banking application. 
When users make everyday debit card purchases, the transaction amount is rounded up to the nearest 10 TRY, 
and the spare change is automatically transferred from their primary Checking Account into a designated Savings Goal account.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
* **Young Professionals:** Entering the workforce brings financial independence, but managing discretionary spending while trying to save for major life experiences (e.g., travel, hobbies) is challenging due to a lack of structured saving habits.
* **New Parents:** Transitioning into parenthood brings significant mental load and unexpected daily expenses. Parents feel overwhelmed attempting to manually plan long-term financial milestones (e.g., pre-school costs) for their children.

---

## 3. Business Objectives & Value
* **Customer Retention & Engagement:** Automated "set-and-forget" mechanics increase daily active app engagement and reduce customer churn.
* **Increased Low-Cost Deposit Base:** Keeping micro-savings within internal sub-accounts increases total deposit volume retained by the bank.
* **Product Cross-Selling:** Prepares users to transition into higher-tier investment or term-deposit products once goal milestones are reached.

---

* **Name:** Mert Kaya (Age 32, Marketing Specialist)
* **Goal:** Build an inflation-resistant safety net for his newborn's pre-school enrollment in 5 years.
* **Pain Point:** Overwhelmed by daily parenting responsibilities; forgets to manually transfer money to savings monthly.
* **ForgetMeNot Usage:** Round-up target set to "Efe's Pre-School Fund", recurring fixed booster (+10 TRY extra on weekends).

---

## 5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Adoption Rate:** Achieve 15% adoption among active debit card users within 90 days of launch.
* **Average Monthly Savings per User:** Target an average of 350 TRY rounded up per active user per month.
* **Goal Completion Rate:** >70% of active goals remaining open until at least 80% of target amount is achieved.
* **Churn Rate:** <5% opt-out rate within the first 30 days of enabling the feature.
